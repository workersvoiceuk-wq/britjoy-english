Britjoy English privacy policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026 · Versión en español

Britjoy English is an educational app by Voz Unida Mundo Academy for learning English and practical life in the UK. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use the app (Android, iOS) or its web version.

Summary

  • Your learning progress is stored only on your device (completed lessons, XP, streak, hearts and settings). It is never sent to a server.
  • An account is optional. You only need one for AI mode and Britjoy Plus. We then keep your email address, your Plus end date, your AI credit balance, how many free AI messages you used today and your purchase records.
  • Payments are handled by Stripe. We never see or store your card details.
  • No ad networks, analytics or tracking. Free learners see a few recommendations from Voz Unida Mundo itself (Britjoy Plus, inviting a friend, our website); they use no personal data.
  • AI mode (optional): only if you use it, what you type is sent to our server and to the AI provider (Anthropic) to generate Britjoy's reply.

Account

You can sign in with your email address: we send you a 6-digit code (no password). Accounts are run on Supabase (our server provider). We use your email only to sign you in and to link your purchases and AI credits to you; we do not send marketing emails. You can delete your account at any time in More → Britjoy Plus & your account → Delete my account: this cancels any Britjoy Plus subscription and deletes your email, Plus status, credits and AI usage. We keep purchase records (without your account) as long as the law requires for accounting.

Britjoy Plus and payments

Britjoy Plus (monthly or yearly subscription) and AI credit packs are sold on the website through Stripe Checkout. Stripe processes your payment details under its own privacy policy; we only receive the result (what you bought, the amount, the currency and a customer reference). You can change or cancel Plus at any time from Manage subscription. The Android and iOS apps do not sell anything; a subscription bought on the website also works in the apps when you sign in with the same email.

AI mode

AI mode is optional. When you write to Britjoy in that mode, your message (and the earlier messages of that conversation) is sent to a Voz Unida Mundo server hosted on Supabase and from there to Anthropic's Claude API, which generates the reply. Our server does not store conversation content (it only counts how many messages you send, to apply the free daily messages, Plus and credits); Anthropic processes data under its own privacy policy and commercial terms. The conversation is not saved in the app and disappears when you leave the section. Do not type personal data (passwords, passport, account numbers or your share code). AI answers can be wrong: for rules, money, health or paperwork, always check the official source.

Britjoy's voice

The app reads text aloud using your own device's or browser's speech synthesiser.

Speaking exercises and the microphone

In speaking exercises, the app asks for microphone access only when you tap the microphone button. It uses your browser's or device's speech recognition to turn what you say into text and compares it with the sentence on the device. The app does not record or keep your voice; depending on your browser (for example Chrome), the audio may be processed by the browser maker's speech service under its own policy. You can always skip the exercise with “I can't speak right now”.

Links and videos

The app links to external websites (for example GOV.UK, NHS, TfL, British Council or Busuu). If you open them, their privacy policies apply. Videos are bundled with the app and are not loaded from social networks.

Children

The app can be used without an account. Accounts, AI mode and purchases are meant for adults (18+); children should use the app without signing in.

Deleting your data

Use Ajustes → Borrar mi progreso (Settings → Delete my progress) to remove all progress. It is also removed when you uninstall the app or clear your browser data.

General information, not advice

The "Vivir en UK" section gives general information checked against official sources. It is not legal, immigration or financial advice; always confirm on the official website before making decisions.
